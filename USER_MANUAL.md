## 1. Getting Started

### Visiting the Website

Open your browser and navigate to the MecBill Tech Salon website. The homepage showcases our services, featured products, and latest blog posts.

### Creating an Account

1. Click **Sign Up** in the top navigation bar
2. Fill in your details:
   - Full name
   - Email address
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Password (minimum 8 characters)
3. Click **Create Account**
4. You'll receive a confirmation — you're ready to go!

Alternatively, you can sign up using your **Google account** for one-click registration.

### Signing In

1. Click **Sign In** in the top navigation bar
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **Sign In**

If you've forgotten your password:
1. Click **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for a 6-digit verification code
4. Enter the code and set a new password

## 3. Browsing & Shopping

Click **Shop** in the main navigation to browse our product catalog.

- **Hair Care** — Shampoos, conditioners, treatments
- **Styling** — Gels, creams, oils, sprays

1. Use the **search bar** at the top of the shop page
2. Type a product name or keyword

Click any product to view its detail page:

- **Description** — Detailed product information
- **Price** — Current price in Nigerian Naira (₦)

### Writing Reviews

For products you've purchased:
1. Go to the product page
2. Click **Write a Review**
3. Select your star rating (1-5 stars)
4. Write your review
5. Click **Submit**

> Note: You can only review products you've purchased and received.

## 4. Shopping Cart & Checkout

### Adding Items to Cart

1. On a product page, select the quantity you want
2. Click **Add to Cart**
3. The cart icon in the navigation updates with the item count

### Managing Your Cart

Click the **cart icon** in the navigation to open the cart drawer:

- **Change quantity** — Use the +/- buttons
- **Remove items** — Click the X button next to an item
- **Apply coupon** — Enter your coupon code and click Apply
- **View total** — See subtotal, shipping, discounts, and total

### Checkout Process

1. Click **Proceed to Checkout** from your cart
2. **Shipping Address** — Enter or confirm your delivery address
3. **Payment Method** — Choose one:
   - **Card (Stripe)** — Pay with debit/credit card
   - **Bank Transfer (Paystack)** — Pay via bank transfer
   - **Pay on Delivery** — Pay when your order arrives
4. **Coupon Code** — Apply a discount code (if available)
5. **Loyalty Points** — Use your points for a discount (optional)
6. Review your order summary
7. Click **Place Order**

### Coupon Codes

- Enter your coupon code in the checkout page
- Click **Apply**
- The discount will be reflected in your total
- Note: Coupons and loyalty points cannot be used together on the same order

### Free Shipping

Orders over **₦30,000** qualify for **free shipping**. Standard shipping is ₦2,000.

## 5. Payments

### Paying by Card (Stripe)

1. Select **Card (Stripe)** at checkout
2. Click **Place Order**
3. Enter your card details in the secure Stripe form:
   - Card number
   - Expiry date
   - CVC
4. Click **Pay**
5. You'll be redirected to the order confirmation page

### Paying by Bank Transfer (Paystack)

1. Select **Bank Transfer (Paystack)** at checkout
2. Click **Place Order**
3. You'll be redirected to Paystack's secure payment page
4. Select your bank and follow the instructions
5. Complete the transfer
6. You'll be redirected back to the order confirmation page

### Pay on Delivery

1. Select **Pay on Delivery** at checkout
2. Click **Place Order**
3. Your order will be processed
4. Pay the delivery person when your order arrives
5. Accepted: Cash or bank transfer

### Payment Security

- All card payments are processed through **Stripe** — your card details never touch our servers
- Bank transfers are processed through **Paystack** — a PCI-certified payment processor
- All transactions are encrypted and secure

## 6. Order Tracking

### Viewing Your Orders

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **My Orders**
2. See a list of all your orders with:
   - Order number
   - Date placed
   - Status badge
   - Total amount
   - Payment method

### Order Status Explained

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Pending** | Order received, awaiting payment confirmation |
| **Processing** | Payment confirmed, order is being prepared |
| **Shipped** | Order has been dispatched for delivery |
| **Delivered** | Order has been delivered to you |
| **Cancelled** | Order was cancelled |
| **Returned** | Order was returned |

### Order Timeline

Click any order to see its detailed timeline:

```
✓ Order Placed          — Jan 15, 2026 at 10:30 AM
✓ Payment Confirmed     — Jan 15, 2026 at 10:32 AM
✓ Processing            — Jan 15, 2026 at 2:00 PM
○ Shipped               — (pending)
○ Delivered             — (pending)
```

### Tracking Number

When your order ships, a tracking number will appear on the order detail page. Use this to track your delivery.

### Cancelling an Order

You can cancel an order while it's still in **Pending** or **Processing** status:

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **My Orders**
2. Find the order you want to cancel
3. Click **Cancel**
4. Confirm the cancellation

> Note: Orders that have already been shipped cannot be cancelled. Please contact support instead.

## 7. Booking Appointments

### Browsing Services

1. Click **Book** in the main navigation
2. Browse service categories:
   - **Braids** — Box braids, cornrows, goddess braids
   - **Weaves & Wigs** — Installations, closures, frontal
   - **Natural Hair** — Twists, locs, silk press
   - **Coloring** — Full color, highlights, balayage
   - **Treatments** — Deep conditioning, keratin, scalp treatment

### Selecting a Service

1. Click a service category
2. Browse available services within that category
3. Click a service to see:
   - Description and what's included
   - Duration estimate
   - Price range
   - Available stylists

### Choosing a Stylist

1. On the service page, view available stylists
2. Each stylist shows:
   - Profile photo
   - Name and specialization
   - Rating
   - Brief bio
3. Select your preferred stylist, or choose **Any Available Stylist**

> Note: Some stylists may be unavailable on certain dates. Incompatible stylists will show a warning message.

### Scheduling Your Appointment

1. Select a **date** from the calendar
2. Select an available **time slot**
3. Review your booking summary:
   - Service name
   - Stylist name
   - Date and time
   - Duration
   - Price
4. Click **Confirm Booking**

### Appointment Confirmation

After booking:
- You'll see a confirmation page with your booking reference
- A confirmation email is sent to your address
- The appointment appears in your dashboard

### Rescheduling

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **My Appointments**
2. Find the appointment you want to reschedule
3. Click **Reschedule**
4. Select a new date and time
5. Confirm the change

### Cancelling an Appointment

1. Go to **Dashboard** → **My Appointments**
2. Find the appointment you want to cancel
3. Click **Cancel**
4. Enter a reason (optional)
5. Confirm the cancellation

> Note: Cancellation policies may apply. Check our cancellation policy for details.

## 9. Loyalty Points

### Earning Points

You earn **1 loyalty point for every ₦100 spent** on completed orders.

Points are automatically added to your account after your order is marked as **Delivered**.

### Point Tiers

| Tier | Points Required | Benefits |
|------|----------------|----------|
| **Bronze** | 0 - 499 | Base tier |
| **Silver** | 500 - 1,499 | 5% bonus on all orders |
| **Gold** | 1,500 - 4,999 | 10% bonus on all orders |
| **Platinum** | 5,000+ | 15% bonus on all orders |

### Redeeming Points

1. At checkout, toggle **Use Loyalty Points**
2. You can use up to **50% of your order total** in points
3. Points are redeemed at a rate of **1 point = ₦1**
4. The discount is applied to your order total

> Note: You cannot use both a coupon code and loyalty points on the same order.

### Viewing Your Balance

Go to **Dashboard** → **Loyalty Points** to see:
- Current balance
- Points earned history
- Points redeemed history
- Your current tier

## 14. FAQ & Support

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Go to Dashboard → My Orders → click on your order to see the timeline and tracking number.

**Q: Can I cancel my order?**
A: Yes, if the order is still in Pending or Processing status. Go to My Orders and click Cancel.

**Q: How do I use a coupon code?**
A: Enter the code at checkout and click Apply. Note that coupons cannot be combined with loyalty points.

**Q: How do I earn loyalty points?**
A: You earn 1 point for every ₦100 spent on completed orders. Points are added automatically after delivery.

**Q: Can I change my appointment time?**
A: Yes, go to Dashboard → My Appointments → find the appointment → click Reschedule.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept debit/credit cards (Stripe), bank transfers (Paystack), and cash on delivery.

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes. All card payments are processed through Stripe and bank transfers through Paystack — both are PCI-certified. We never store your card details.
